# Public course audit — 14 September 2026

This is a dated audit record, not a guarantee that every statement is error-free
or that linked documentation will remain unchanged.

## Coverage

- All 42 course chapters: 354 learning sections, 307 multiple-choice checks and
  120 exercises, including explanations, deeper material and model answers.
- Global glossary: 72 rows, plus chapter-specific definitions and reading notes.
- Diagrams: 205 original placements reviewed; 204 remain after removing the
  premature timestamp diagram from the Data and features terminology section.
  Forty-five visual definitions were corrected or clarified. The registry now
  has 180 definitions, including separate interview-response and operational
  incident timelines.
- Reading links: 239 unique URLs checked. After correcting a Kaggle lesson URL,
  238 resolved successfully; one Cloudflare response was access-blocked (403),
  not established to be broken. HEAD errors were confirmed with GET where needed.

## Material corrections

Diagrams follow their explanatory text or exercise prompt. Examples identify
their domain and define terms such as the scoring service. Teaching labels no
longer repeatedly fade out. The mobile viewport is explicit.

Technical repairs include prediction-time and feature-availability semantics,
late labels, parity-test limits, replay and duplicate safety, Kubernetes scaling,
GPU allocation, SQL isolation, quorum/Raft rules, tail latency, ranking arithmetic,
delivery acknowledgements, MLflow/GitOps responsibilities, reproducibility,
solver status, uncertainty, and ML/LLM evaluation. Failure behavior is tied to
the service's harm model rather than universal fail-open advice. Shadow traffic
and prompt-injection defenses no longer imply zero risk or prompt-only security.

Candidate-specific assumptions were removed from public explanations and model
answers. Company-specific preparation and owner authentication are not part of
the public course.

## Progress and verification boundaries

The public site has no sign-in or cloud configuration. Completion, quiz choices
and free-form answers stay in the browser; export/import provides portability.
All existing topic, namespaced section and exercise IDs, quiz answer positions,
and option counts were checked against the preceding revision and retained.
Some corrected question/option wording changes meaning without changing its
saved position. Existing completion marks are retained, not proof that a reader
has reviewed the corrected material.

Independent counterpart reviews followed implementation. Syntax, generated-page,
public-output allowlist, import/export, cloud-adapter compatibility, initial
hydration and course-switch synthetic checks passed. Synthetic state tests do
not substitute for an actual second-device test or guarantee network delivery.
Rendered Chrome checks covered lesson order, diagram text bounds, and phone-width
layout. Long diagrams scroll within their cards rather than widening the page.
