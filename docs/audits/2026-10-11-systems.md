## Evidence and corrections

- SQL sql-0, sql-f2, sql-f5, sql-f6, sql-f9 and glossary: qualify suffix-only index use, index-only visibility checks, row versus table locks, and migration lock/rollback boundaries. [PostgreSQL multicolumn indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/indexes-multicolumn.html), [index-only scans](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/indexes-index-only-scans.html), [locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/explicit-locking.html).
- Networking nb-0, nb-f4: distinguish UDP transport retransmission from resolver retry and TLS 1.2 from TLS 1.3 resumption. [TLS 1.3 RFC](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8446.html).
- cache-0/cache-1 and sdf-a2: distinguish hot-key skew, database-managed cache configuration, expiry age and declared durability. [Redis eviction guidance](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/reference/eviction/). Database buffer pools are configuration, not application-maintained caches.
- apc-f1: a stable cursor avoids positional shifts, but does not create a consistent snapshot during arbitrary sort-key mutation.
- npi-f2: distinguish Bigtable sparse qualifiers from Cassandra CQL typed declared schemas. [Cassandra CQL schema](https://cassandra.apache.org/doc/stable/cassandra/developing/cql/ddl.html).
- dc-6b: atomic commit and isolation are separate; sagas need explicit intermediate-state semantics.
- qs-f6: correct fencing anchor to dc-7. qs-a3: producer append order and serial effects are assumptions; partition keys do not create business order across writers. [Kafka design and guarantees](https://kafka.apache.org/41/design/design/).
- qs-a1: reservation expiry/payment confirmation must race through atomic state transitions; unknown and late payment outcomes require reconciliation. [Idempotent consumer pattern](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/idempotent-consumer).
- sr-f2: remove repeated variant caveat; scope unsaturated TF-IDF claim to raw-count variant.
- sdc-0: remove incorrect fixed count. sdc-10: measure latency stages, authorize retrieval/cache, select eligible models, abstain without evidence, and explain that retraction cannot undo disclosure.
